# Page review record

Copy the relevant sections for a substantial audit or rewrite. Keep the record outside public navigation, for example under `docs/editorial/reviews/`. For a small edit, a short PR description is sufficient. Replace prompts with evidence; omit inapplicable sections with a reason when needed.

## Page brief

- Page(s) and current revision:
- Request mode and scope: audit, rewrite, or create
- Primary audience and assumed knowledge:
- Reader's goal or question:
- Page pattern and observable success criterion:
- Constraints to preserve: supported paths, exact identifiers, URLs/anchors, costs/consequences

## Audit and disposition

| Priority | Location and evidence | Reader impact | Proposed action | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Prioritize blockers to completing the task, then avoidable confusion, then polish. Dispositions are accepted/resolved, rejected with rationale, or deferred with an owner. Separate verified behavior from editorial judgment and unknowns; do not invent usability scores.

## Source and media evidence

| Claim or asset | Authoritative source / asset path | Revision or capture date | Verified scope / gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

For missing captures, name the exact screen/action, why it helps, the intended caption, and the owner. Distinguish real captures, illustrative content, and observed output. Never fill unknown metadata with today's date by default.

## Validation

- Build and links: command, result, candidate revision
- Browser: pages, viewports, interactions, and evidence
- Independent review: reviewer, findings, dispositions, candidate revision
- Installation/E2E test: actual scope/result or not performed
- Intended-audience test: participant/task/observations or not performed; do not record identity unless needed
- Remaining gaps and owners:

## Delivery

State which work is edited, committed, pushed, reviewed, merged, deployed, or live-verified. Record pending publication actions separately. An editing workflow does not grant permission to contact readers, create a PR, or publish changes.
